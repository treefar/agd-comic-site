GraduationDoes an unmatched completed course always need to be retaken?＋

Not necessarily. The guide notes that credit transfer, substitute courses or placement-based course exemptions may not yet be matched. Take approval and grade records to your department or registration office. Do not duplicate courses yourself; discontinued required courses need the offering unit’s designated substitute.
