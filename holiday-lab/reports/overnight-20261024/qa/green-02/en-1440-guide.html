Graduation Audit

First check your academic entry year and class type. Credits, required courses, English proficiency, certifications, internships, and capstone project must all be verified separately.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Check the rules for your entry year and program. Ask the department about making up or recognizing anything missing.

Step details

  1. 01

    Find the curriculum roadmap for your academic entry year

    Find 'Department of Animation and Game Design' in the Office of Academic Affairs curriculum catalog across academic years, and check your enrollment year as well as regular class or skills-excellence class (技優班). Save your copy to reference later during course selection and graduation audits.

  2. 02

    Cross-check with the system graduation audit to list missing requirements

    In the university portal, open Academic Affairs → Queries → Graduation Credit Audit. List missing courses, English requirements, certificates, internships and capstone work separately. Check which semester the audit includes, and check required zero-credit courses as well as the credit total.

  3. 03

    Bring the audit results to the department office to confirm makeup courses or credit recognition

    When visiting the department office, bring your enrollment year curriculum, grades, and screenshots of unpassed items. Ask directly which course to take to make up for missing credits and how existing certificates can be recognized. If credit transfer or student status are involved, ask the Registration and Curriculum Division for assistance.

Please Note skills-excellence class (技優班) requirements are audited separately; please bring your academic entry year curriculum roadmap to the department office for confirmation.

Off-Campus Internship

First confirm whether the job duties are eligible for credit recognition before handling faculty mentoring, contracts, and insurance, avoiding finding out after completion that it does not qualify as an internship.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

These are preparations before starting an internship. Also ask how to contact your supervisor during it and what to submit afterward.

Step details

  1. 01

    First obtain current internship regulations from the department office

    First inform the department office of your enrollment year, class section, and expected internship period to obtain the applicable regulations and application forms. Also ask how hours are calculated and what must be submitted upon completion, rather than only knowing the minimum required hours.

  2. 02

    Confirm the internship organization, job duties, and faculty advisor

    Organize the company name, job description, planned tasks, and work period for the department office or your advisor to review. Confirm in advance whether self-found companies or existing part-time jobs can be recognized; do not wait until completion to ask.

  3. 03

    Complete contract and insurance confirmations before starting according to regulations

    Ask the internship coordinator to confirm who signs the contract, when insurance takes effect, and whom to contact if problems arise. Set the start date only after receiving the checklist of required supplementary documents; never treat a company's verbal agreement as completed school procedures.

Please Note For self-sourced companies, recognizing part-time work hours, or terminating an internship, please check applicable regulations with the department office first.

Certification Recognition

Whether a certification fulfills graduation requirements must first be checked against the department list; recognition toward graduation requirements and financial awards or subsidies are two separate matters, and application procedures must still be checked after passing.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Graduation recognition and awards are separate. Check the current eligibility and deadline for awards.

Step details

  1. 01

    Ask the department office whether it is recognized before taking the certification exam

    Submit the full certification title, issuing agency, and planned exam version to the department office to verify if it applies to your enrollment year and class section. Confirm it is eligible for recognition before arranging registration and payment; do not simply take a test because others passed it.

  2. 02

    Complete recognition registration after obtaining the certificate

    Prepare clear copies of your certificate or score transcript, checking your name, certificate title, and effective date first. Then confirm with the department office where to register it, whether additional documents are needed, and when the result will appear in the graduation audit.

  3. 03

    Separately check subsidy eligibility with the Career Development Center and submit application

    First check with the Career Development Center regarding current reward eligibility and ensure your remittance account details have been submitted to the Cashier Section. Even if an instructor registered the certification as a group, still confirm whether an additional reward application is required.

Please Note skills-excellence class (技優班) requirements are audited separately; please verify graduation recognition and subsidy eligibility with the department office and Career Development Center respectively.

I Want to Apply for Leave

After submitting a leave application, follow up on approval results. Leave categories, days, and exam weeks may have different procedures; prepare supporting documents first before checking.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

If exams, extended leave or an emergency are involved, ask the responsible office about additional procedures.

Step details

  1. 01

    Confirm leave type, dates, and required supporting documents

    Organize the dates, class periods, and reasons for your absence, then verify the leave category and required proof with your class advisor or the Student Assistance Section. For exam periods, mental health leave, or extended absences, ask clearly in advance whether additional procedures apply.

  2. 02

    Submit application in the university administration system

    Log into the leave application service via the school's 'Current Students' portal, fill in the details in the system, and confirm submission. If you cannot find the required option, bring your screen to consult your advisor or the Student Assistance Section; do not assume notifying someone via messaging apps completes the process.

  3. 03

    Track approvals; check with the coordinator if exams or extended leave are involved

    After submitting, return to the system to verify whether it has been approved; if there is no response, follow up proactively with your advisor. If it affects exams or involves extended leave, consult your advisor and the Registration and Curriculum Division for follow-up steps; do not mistake submission for approval.

Please Note For exams, extended leave, or retroactive leave applications, check the procedure with your class advisor first; for exam handling, contact the Registration and Curriculum Division.

Course Selection and Add/Drop

First check your official class schedule in the Academic Information System. You must confirm required courses, schedule conflicts, and credit totals yourself; attending a class does not mean you have completed the add process.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Recheck both registration and course changes. A past schedule does not mean late registration is still available.

Step details

  1. 01

    Check required courses, credit totals, and class schedules

    Place your enrollment year curriculum and this semester's system schedule side by side. Check first for any missed required courses, then verify total credits and timetable conflicts. If you have credit transfers, retaken courses, or makeup courses, list them separately and consult the department office before course selection.

  2. 02

    Complete preliminary course selection or add/drop according to current announcements

    Check with the Office of Academic Affairs whether the current period is preliminary registration, add/drop, or course withdrawal, and follow that stage's announcement. Past add/drop dates cannot be reused; if past deadlines or facing special circumstances, consult the Registration and Curriculum Division first.

  3. 03

    Reopen the system to verify results; contact the Registration and Curriculum Division if any issues arise

    After making changes, reopen your system schedule to verify that course titles, class sections, and times are indeed correct. If anything differs from expected, note the course code, take a screenshot of the issue, and contact the Registration and Curriculum Division; attending class or receiving instructor consent cannot substitute for official system records.

Credit Transfer

Having taken similar courses does not mean credits are automatically transferred. Prepare original transcripts and the application form for sequential review by the offering department, the degree program, and the Office of Academic Affairs.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓
  3. ↓
  4. ↓

Check your eligibility and the current deadline first. Make course changes yourself after the initial review; do not wait for the final decision.

Step details

  1. 01

    Complete the online application and print it

    Check your eligibility and the current deadline, then complete and print the form in the university system. Prepare the original transcript from your previous institution and any applicable evidence together.

  2. 02

    Request initial review by the course unit and your department heads

    Submit your documents to the heads of the course-offering unit and your own department. Use the initial review to check which courses may receive credit.

  3. 03

    Make course changes yourself based on the initial review

    If a course approved at the initial review is still on your timetable, drop it yourself and check what you need to add. Do not wait for the final decision to start making changes.

  4. 04

    Submit the form and evidence to Registration and Curriculum Division

    Submit the initially reviewed form and evidence to Registration and Curriculum Division as required. Prepare all documents together and submit them within the current application period.

  5. 05

    Return to the system to check the final decision

    After submission, check the final decision in the university system and compare it with your timetable and graduation check. Bring any discrepancies to Registration and Curriculum Division.

Capstone project

First confirm your cohort's regulations with your advisor and the department office before scheduling production, reviews, and submission of deliverables. Completing the project work does not mean all procedures are completed.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Get your cohort’s documents before planning groups, review dates, work formats and exhibition requirements.

Step details

  1. 01

    Obtain your cohort's regulations and forms from the department office

    Confirm with the department office which capstone project regulations apply to your cohort, and ask for all forms regarding team formation, team changes, reviews, and makeup reviews at once. Check the version and target audience first; do not directly reuse files handed down from seniors.

  2. 02

    Schedule review and submission timelines with your advisor

    Present your current work progress and to-do list to your advisor, asking what content must be presented at each review. Then schedule your production and document preparation backwards according to cohort announcements, leaving room for revisions rather than guessing deadlines on your own.

  3. 03

    Submit works, documentation, and exhibition outcomes according to regulations

    Before submission, have your advisor confirm whether the work, documentation, exhibition materials, and authorization forms are all required, checking item by item against designated formats. After submitting, verify receipt by the coordinator and whether supplementary files are needed; finishing the work does not mean official procedures are complete.

Please Note For team formation, grading, supplementary reviews, and graduation exhibition schedules, please obtain your cohort's official regulations from the department office before planning.

English Graduation Requirements

English/foreign language coursework and English graduation requirements must be checked separately. First check your entry year and placement results, then look up eligible certifications or alternative pathways.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Check English course credits separately from proficiency requirements. If placement exempts you from a course, ask how to make up the required credits.

Step details

  1. 01

    Check your entry year, placement level, and foreign language credits

    Retrieve your freshman English placement results as well as completed or waived English and foreign language courses, checking them against your curriculum. If granted a course waiver, confirm with the Center for General Education which foreign language credits still need to be made up; do not assume an exemption means credits are automatically fulfilled.

  2. 02

    Confirm current graduation requirements and alternative pathways with language program coordinators

    Explain your enrollment year and current grades to the Center for General Education to find the language program coordinator. Before registering for proficiency tests, verify acceptable tests, score thresholds, certificate validity periods, and alternative options; do not apply standards from other schools or scholarship programs.

  3. 03

    After completing courses and certificate accreditation, check graduation audit records

    Organize your course grades and the certificates required by the language coordinator separately, asking clearly where to submit them and how the submitted grades and certificates are recognized. Check your graduation audit afterward; if it still shows unpassed, bring your submission records to verify with the coordinator.

Please Note First confirm test scores, certificate validity periods, and alternative pathways with language program coordinators; certification subsidy score criteria cannot be directly used as graduation requirements.

Leave of Absence, Return to Study and Military Service

Formal procedures must be completed for a leave of absence and return to study; check military service details separately with the Student Assistance Section. Contact coordinators in advance to avoid merely stopping class attendance without properly managing your student status.

Steps and details

Choose what you need

Apply for leave or return according to your situation. Students subject to military service must also check with Student Assistance Section; academic-status procedures do not replace military-service procedures.

Details for each option

  1. A

    Leave of absence: contact your department, then collect the form

    Arrange a time with your adviser or department office, then register and collect the form from Registration and Curriculum Division. Complete the interviews, clearance and document return as announced; the form-collection date affects refund calculations.

  2. B

    Return or extended leave: reply and proceed as notified

    When you receive a return notice, reply by its deadline to return or extend your leave, and confirm next semester’s registration. If military service is involved, ask Registration and Curriculum Division which evidence is required.

  3. C

    Military service: check separately with Student Assistance Section

    When transferring, returning or extending your studies, check your military-service records and any deferment or reserve-call procedures separately with Student Assistance Section. Completing academic-status procedures does not complete military-service procedures.

Please Note Individual conscription deferments, reserve recall deferments, and military document deadlines must be determined by the coordinator; completing student status procedures does not mean military records are finalized.

Scholarships, Financial Aid, and Emergency Assistance

For financial hardship or sudden family emergencies, check first with the Student Assistance Section for applicable programs. Eligibility, documents, and deadlines vary; there is no need to wait until you are overwhelmed before asking.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
