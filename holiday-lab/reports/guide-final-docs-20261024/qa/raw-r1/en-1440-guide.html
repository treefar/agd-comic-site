I Want to Apply for Leave

Check the leave type and application deadline, prepare the documents, submit online, and track final approval. During exam week, also confirm make-up exam procedures.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓

Check the application deadline for the leave type → apply online with supporting documents → check final approval. The five-day approval period does not allow you to delay your application by five days.

Step details

  1. 01

    Confirm leave type, dates, and required supporting documents

    List the dates, class periods, and reason. The rules revised on November 19, 2025 require personal leave within three days after the event, sick leave within 14 days, and official leave in advance. Psychological leave allows five days per semester; two or more days require documentation. During exam week, all types except menstrual leave require documentation.

  2. 02

    Submit application in the university administration system

    Apply through the University Information System and upload the required documents. The Student Assistance Section’s leave page provides instructions and official-leave and personal-leave proof forms. Have the organizer sign or stamp official-leave proof before uploading it; an event photo or a message alone is insufficient.

  3. 03

    Track approvals; check with the coordinator if exams or extended leave are involved

    Check the approval status in the system. Under the rules, ordinary leave is treated as declined if the advisor or department chair has not acted more than five days after submission, including holidays. For official leave, an advisor’s inaction counts as consent at that stage, but further approval is required. Ask the Student Assistance Section at ext. 2126 about exams or longer absences.

Please Note The application deadline and the approval deadline are different. For official leave, an advisor’s inaction after five days counts as consent at that stage; further approvals are still required. Confirm exam and make-up exam procedures separately.

Credit Transfer

Having taken similar courses does not mean credits are automatically transferred. Prepare original transcripts and the application form for sequential review by the offering department, the degree program, and the Office of Academic Affairs.

Steps and details

Process overview

  1. ↓
  2. ↓
  3. ↓
  4. ↓

Check your eligibility and the current deadline first. Make course changes yourself after the initial review; do not wait for the final decision.

Step details

  1. 01

    Complete the online application and print it

    Check your qualifying status or reason and the current deadline, then enter, review and print the application from the university system. Prepare the original full transcript from your previous school. Use the system-generated form, not an old blank form or handbook screenshot.

  2. 02

    Request initial review by the course unit and your department heads

    Submit your documents to the heads of the course-offering unit and your own department. Use the initial review to check which courses may receive credit.

  3. 03

    Make course changes yourself based on the initial review

    If a course approved at the initial review is still on your timetable, drop it yourself and check what you need to add. Do not wait for the final decision to start making changes.

  4. 04

    Submit the form and evidence to Registration and Curriculum Division

    Submit the initially reviewed form and evidence to Registration and Curriculum Division as required. Prepare all documents together and submit them within the current application period.

  5. 05

    Return to the system to check the final decision

    After submission, check the final decision in the university system and compare it with your timetable and graduation check. Bring any discrepancies to Registration and Curriculum Division.

Please Note Credit transfer applications for 115-1 closed on 21 September. Current rules allow one application per qualifying status or reason. The handbook screenshots are older, and the current generated form still needs confirmation.

Leave of Absence, Return to Study and Military Service

Formal procedures must be completed for a leave of absence and return to study; check military service details separately with the Student Assistance Section. Contact coordinators in advance to avoid merely stopping class attendance without properly managing your student status.

Steps and details

Choose what you need

Apply for leave or return according to your situation. Students subject to military service must also check with Student Assistance Section; academic-status procedures do not replace military-service procedures.

Details for each option

  1. A

    Leave of absence: contact your department, then collect the form

    Arrange a meeting with your advisor or department first, then bring your student ID to collect the form from the Registration and Curriculum Division. Complete clearance and return it within seven days including the registration day. A late submission requires a new form and changes the refund reference date. Leave takes effect only after approval.

  2. B
