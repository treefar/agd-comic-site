  - paragraph: The Office of Academic Affairs provides academic services to all faculty and students. It consists of Registration and Curriculum Division, Admissions and Selection Division, which are in charge of student enrollment, and academic affairs pertinent to both students and teachers. Pursuant to School Policy Purposes, Building a High-Quality Educational Services, Creating Effective Teaching and Learning Environments, Promote Holistic Teaching and Learning Development, In pursuit of a campus of “Academic, Intellectual, Happy, Hopeful”.
